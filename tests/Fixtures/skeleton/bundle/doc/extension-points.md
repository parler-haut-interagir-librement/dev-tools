# Extension points

The package is at `0.x`: its extension points are stable only from its 1.0 release. Until then, a minor version may
change them, and its CHANGELOG says how.

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Replace a service | Alias the interface the bundle registers to your own service |
| Add to the bundle | Tag a service with a tag this page lists; the bundle collects every service so tagged |
